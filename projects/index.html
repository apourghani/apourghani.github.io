Research papers

Free Lunch Uncertainty: Sequence-Level Rényi Entropy from Particle Decoding for Adaptive LLM Reasoning

We estimate uncertainty over an entire LLM answer from statistics already computed during particle decoding. Using sequence-level Rényi entropy to decide when to allocate more particles matched the 64-particle baseline’s accuracy on MATH500 while using 34.2% fewer particles.

Quotient-Categorical Representations for Bellman-Compatible Average-Reward Distributional Reinforcement Learning

We use a quotient-categorical representation to model differential returns in average-reward reinforcement learning. It respects the common-shift ambiguity of the bias, and we prove convergence of the resulting temporal-difference updates even when the average reward is estimated online.

Learning to Control Coupled-Dynamics Environments with Joint Markov Decision Processes

We develop optimal-control methods for environments where different actions share the same randomness. Joint Markov decision processes preserve the dependence between these counterfactual outcomes, and we prove when Bellman updates recover their joint return distribution.
