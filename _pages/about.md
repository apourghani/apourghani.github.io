I’m Aliasghar Pourghani, named after my grandfather, but my friends call me **Amin**, and you can too. The “A” sounds like the “a” in *at*, and the “i” sounds like “ee” in *see*.

I was born in Salmas, a small town near Iran’s northwestern border. Since then, my journey has taken me to Tehran, where I studied Electrical Engineering and minored in Applied Mathematics at Sharif University of Technology, and then to Purdue University for my PhD, co-advised by Prof. Gupta and Prof. Hashemi.

My background brings together mathematics, engineering, and machine learning. My research interests include reinforcement learning, LLMs post training, optimization, and imitation learning.

Outside research, I enjoy listening to music, playing guitar, reading books, watching series, and spending time with friends. I speak Azerbaijani, Persian, and English, so feel free to say hello in any of them.

This website is a place for my research, projects, and occasional thoughts. Some things here will be finished; others will still be taking shape. That seems like a fair introduction to research—and to me.
